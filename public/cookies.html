Cookie Policy

Effective: May 1, 2026 Last updated: September 4, 2026

Summary — The client uses browser or device storage for authentication, wallet/session state, preferences, and caches. Configured analytics and error-monitoring providers may also process client events. We do not use advertising cookies.

01What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored by your browser. localStorage is similar — key-value data scoped to our domain. Both let the Service remember things between visits.

02Cookies we set

Authentication and infrastructure providers may set first-party or operational cookies. Exact names and durations can vary by deployed client and hosting configuration; inspect your browser's storage panel for the current values. We do not intentionally set advertising cookies.

03Local storage we use

Local storage can contain wallet/session state, authentication state, preferences, feature progress, and response caches. These values are local application records and are not themselves on-chain transactions.

04Third-party services

Infrastructure and configured providers can include Cloudflare, Solana RPC/Helius, Firebase Analytics, and Sentry. They may process cookies, device identifiers, request metadata, product events, or diagnostics under their own policies and configuration.

05Managing cookies

You can disable or clear cookies and local storage in your browser. Authentication, wallet/session continuity, preferences, or cached features may stop working or reset.

06Do Not Track

Browser DNT signals are not a substitute for the storage and provider controls described above. Use browser privacy controls and any consent controls exposed by the deployed client.

07Changes & contact

If storage or provider practices materially change, this page will be updated.
